# Legal Jurisdiction Skill

Determine the likely jurisdiction from:
- forum;
- parties;
- location of conduct;
- governing-law terms;
- federal statutory basis;
- agency involvement;
- treaty/international facts.

Return likely and alternative jurisdictions plus confidence and missing facts.

Do not silently choose a jurisdiction when the facts are materially ambiguous.
