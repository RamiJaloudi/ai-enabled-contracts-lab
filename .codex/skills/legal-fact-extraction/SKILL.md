# Legal Fact Extraction Skill

Extract:
- actors and relationships;
- dates and chronology;
- locations;
- conduct;
- communications;
- notice;
- intent or knowledge;
- injury/damages;
- procedural events;
- documents;
- disputed facts;
- undisputed facts;
- allegations.

Do not convert an allegation into an established fact.
