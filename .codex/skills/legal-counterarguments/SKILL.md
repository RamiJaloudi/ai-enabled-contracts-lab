# Legal Counterargument Skill

For each legal theory:
1. Identify missing elements.
2. Identify contradictory evidence.
3. Identify affirmative defenses.
4. Identify privileges and immunities.
5. Identify procedural bars.
6. Identify jurisdictional objections.
7. Identify alternative factual characterizations.
8. Identify uncertainty.

Do not generate straw-man counterarguments.
