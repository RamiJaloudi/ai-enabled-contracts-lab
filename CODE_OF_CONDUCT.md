# Community Guidelines

AI-Enabled Contracts Lab is an educational open-source project. Participation should be
welcoming, evidence-based, and respectful.

Expected behavior includes constructive technical feedback, patience with
learners, respect for different backgrounds, and careful handling of legal or
confidential material. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate
disclosure of private data, and misleading claims about legal advice are not
acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when necessary to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to
the repository owner through the contact method published on the GitHub profile.
Do not include confidential contract text in a report.
